Before we build

A few things you might want to know.

Can you integrate our existing SaaS product with HighLevel?

Yes. We start with your product’s API and the experience your customers need in HighLevel, then scope the data connections, actions, and interface.

Can you build a HighLevel marketplace app?

Yes. We build marketplace applications and assist with installation flows, testing, and submission. HighLevel controls marketplace review and approval.

Can you extend the HighLevel frontend?

Yes. We build custom JavaScript extensions, embedded tools, and interfaces. We assess the platform’s available extension points and agree how the experience will be maintained.

How do you determine project scope and cost?

We review your requirements, technical constraints, and launch goals before proposing scope and pricing. Start with a project-fit conversation.

Do you support integrations after launch?

Yes. Launch assistance, documentation, maintenance, and ongoing development can be included. The support scope is agreed for each engagement.
