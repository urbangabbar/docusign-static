LC Bridge product · integration & frontend

DocuSign Integration

The challenge: Bring agreements into HighLevel’s contact and workflow experience.

What we built: Field mapping, signer selection, document previews, signing status, and configured workflow connections. Each mapped field can read from the contact selected for its signer role.

What users can do: Review and send agreements inside HighLevel, then sync mapped signer-entered answers after the agreement completes.

Before we build

Before your project starts.

Can you integrate our existing SaaS product with HighLevel?

Yes. Bring your API documentation and the experience your customers need. We scope the data connections, workflow actions, and interface around your product.

Can you build a HighLevel marketplace app?

Yes. We build marketplace applications and assist with installation flows, testing, and submission. HighLevel controls marketplace review and approval.

How do you determine project scope and cost?

We review the user journey, API capabilities, account boundaries, frontend needs, and launch goals before proposing scope and pricing. A few sentences about your project are enough to start.

How are ownership and handover handled?

Discuss code ownership, repository access, hosting, documentation, and handover with us during scoping so the arrangements are agreed for your engagement.

What happens when HighLevel changes?

We assess platform dependencies and maintenance needs during scoping. Post-launch support and ongoing development can be included, with the scope agreed for your project.
