DocuSign Integration capabilities and requirements

For GoHighLevel (GHL), also known as HighLevel.

By LC Bridge editorial team · Updated 12 October 2026

LC Bridge is a third-party DocuSign integration for GoHighLevel. Keep your existing DocuSign account and connect agreements to contact data and configured workflows inside GoHighLevel.

Accounts and plans

You need a GoHighLevel account and your own DocuSign account. LC Bridge and DocuSign subscriptions are separate. Monthly LC Bridge prices are $29 per active sub-account for Starter, $249 for Agency with up to 250 sub-accounts, and $497 for Scale with unlimited sub-accounts. A 14-day trial is available without a card to start. See monthly and annual charges.

What to configure during setup

CapabilitySetup and behavior
Agreement prefillMap GoHighLevel contact values to the intended DocuSign template fields.
Signer-entered answersMap destination contact/custom fields. Values return after the agreement completes.
Multiple recipientsSelect a GoHighLevel contact for each template role and preview resolved fields before sending.
Sending and follow-upConfigure and activate the workflow actions, timing, and account/channel settings you need. Prebuilt workflows start switched off.
Agreement activityView status and agreement activity from the contact record.
Signed PDF accessOpen the completed agreement from its contact context. File attachment depends on storage settings and permissions.
Agency branding and rolloutAgency and Scale support an agency-configured integration experience. Confirm the required branding and deployment settings during setup.

Configure additional sending and reminder tools

The documentation also covers the agreement worklist, reminders, and sending from contact or list contexts. Confirm your template, DocuSign plan, GoHighLevel permissions, enabled workflows, and limits with the setup team before a bulk rollout. A displayed example is not a promise that an action runs without configuration.

Choose the right workflow

Use the workflow setup guide to configure send, follow-up, decline handling, and completion behavior. Use the mapping guide for client answers and the multiple-signers guide for recipient selection.

Evaluate alternatives fairly

GoHighLevel Documents & Contracts is its own signing product. Zapier can connect DocuSign events to LeadConnector contacts and opportunities. Compare the embedded agreement experience, setup ownership, current actions, and full subscription cost for your actual process. See DocuSign versus native signing and connection methods.
