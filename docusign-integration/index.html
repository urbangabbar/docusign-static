Questions

GoHighLevel DocuSign integration FAQ

Does HighLevel (GoHighLevel) have a native DocuSign integration?
HighLevel Documents & Contracts is HighLevel’s own signing product. To keep using your existing DocuSign account inside HighLevel, use a third-party integration such as LC Bridge or configure middleware. LC Bridge’s embedded integration is provided by LC Bridge, not by HighLevel.
How is this different from connecting DocuSign with Zapier, Make, or Pabbly?
Zapier can send DocuSign envelopes and use signing events to update LeadConnector contacts and opportunities. LC Bridge provides an agreement interface in HighLevel with configured field mappings and workflows. Compare the current actions, setup ownership, agency rollout, and full cost for your process. Make and Pabbly capabilities depend on their current connectors and your configuration.
How do I connect DocuSign to GoHighLevel?
Request your 14-day trial, connect your existing DocuSign account, choose a template, map its fields, and preview your first agreement. Configure and activate the workflows you want. Setup help is available, and a call is optional. See the getting-started guide.
Do I need any coding or technical setup?
The integration does not require you to build a custom connector. It still needs an account connection, template field mappings, and workflow configuration. Use the getting-started guide or request setup help.
Will my HighLevel contact fields fill in the document automatically?
Mapped contact values prefill the DocuSign template. Client-entered answers can return to their configured HighLevel contact fields after the agreement completes. Check recipient roles and preview resolved values before sending.
Are DocuSign e-signatures legally binding?
Yes. In the U.S., electronic signatures are legally binding under the ESIGN Act and UETA, and they're valid in most countries. A few document types may still require a wet signature in some jurisdictions.
Can signing a document trigger a HighLevel workflow?
Yes. Configure and activate the completion workflow to update your chosen pipeline stage or start the next action. Follow-up timing, destinations, and channel settings are configured for your account.
Does this work for real estate and insurance agents specifically?
Yes — see our dedicated guides for DocuSign for Real Estate (listing and buyer agreements) and DocuSign for Insurance (applications and ACORD-style forms).
Do I need my own DocuSign account, and how much does it cost?
You connect your existing DocuSign plan; LC Bridge is the integration layer that brings it into HighLevel. Plans start at $29 per active sub-account, with flat-rate Agency ($249/mo, up to 250 sub-accounts) and Scale ($497/mo, unlimited) plans for resellers — and a 14-day free trial on every plan.
Can agencies offer this to their sub-accounts under their own brand?
Agency and Scale plans support an agency-configured integration experience. Confirm branding, account permissions, and rollout settings during setup.
