How to connect DocuSign to GoHighLevel
Browse product documentation

What does LCBridge connect?

LCBridge connects your DocuSign account to GoHighLevel, also known as GoHighLevel or GHL. It lets you send mapped agreements, automate configured follow-ups, and see completed agreement data from the contact record.

Do I need my own DocuSign account?

Yes. You need a GoHighLevel account and your own DocuSign account. LCBridge is the integration; a DocuSign subscription is not included.

Can I send documents prefilled with CRM data?

Yes. Map your GoHighLevel contact fields to the fields in a DocuSign template. Those mapped CRM values populate the agreement when you send it.

Which client answers sync back, and when?

Configured template field mappings can return client-entered details to supported standard or custom contact fields after the agreement completes. For example, a co-buyer’s name and email can go into the primary contact’s custom fields. An individual signer finishing does not mean a multi-signer agreement is complete. Read the multiple-signers guide .

Will agreements and follow-ups send automatically?

They can when you enable and configure the relevant GoHighLevel workflows. Use a pipeline-stage change to send an agreement, a stalled-agreement event for follow-up, and completed signing to advance the opportunity. Choose the stages, timing, and actions for your process.

Where is the signed PDF stored?

You can access the completed PDF from the agreement on the contact record. Storage can be configured for a saved document link, media storage, or contact-file attachment. Contact-file attachment requires the appropriate settings and permissions.

How is this different from GoHighLevel Documents & Contracts?

GoHighLevel has its own Documents & Contracts functionality. LCBridge is for teams that want to keep using DocuSign while connecting it to GoHighLevel. Compare the two approaches .

How does the 14-day trial work?

Complete the trial form below to request assisted setup. We reply the same working day and help connect the integration to your GoHighLevel account. No card is needed to start. A setup call is available if you want one; requesting a trial does not instantly provision an account.

Can my agency offer it under our own brand?

Agency and Scale plans include a white-label integration experience inside your GoHighLevel environment. Branding is configured during setup. Customers still use their own DocuSign account and its signing experience.

What does it cost after the trial?

Monthly billing is $29 per active sub-account for Starter, $249 for Agency with up to 250 sub-accounts, or $497 for Scale with unlimited sub-accounts. Annual billing is available at the rates shown above. All prices are in USD; your DocuSign subscription is separate.
