Before you book

Straight answers about the call.

Who will I actually be speaking with?

Arun, who founded LC Bridge and builds the integration. There is no sales team to hand you off to, so you can ask implementation questions and get a real answer on the call rather than a follow-up email.

Do I need a DocuSign account before we talk?

No. Bring your GoHighLevel setup and the paperwork you send most often. If you don't have DocuSign yet, we'll cover what plan you'd need and whether it's worth it for your volume.

Is this a sales pitch?

It's a working session. If your volume is low enough that the integration won't pay for itself, or you'd be better served by something simpler, you'll hear that on the call.

I only have one quick question.

Then don't book a slot — email arun@lcbridge.app and you'll get a reply. The documentation also covers most setup questions.

Can I just start a trial instead?

Yes. The 14-day trial needs no card and no call. Book a slot only if you'd rather have someone walk your setup with you.
