DocuSign × GoHighLevel (GHL)

DocuSign vs GoHighLevel's built-in signature — which one should you use?

Use GoHighLevel Documents & Contracts when its native document process meets your needs. Use DocuSign with LC Bridge when your team or counterparties already rely on DocuSign and you want its agreements connected to GoHighLevel.

See the comparison See the integration

What's the difference between DocuSign and GoHighLevel's native e-signature?

GoHighLevel Documents & Contracts supports multiple recipients and produces a signed PDF and signature certificate with per-signer activity details. DocuSign is a separate signing platform with its own account, templates, and plan-dependent capabilities. LC Bridge connects that existing DocuSign process to GoHighLevel contact fields and configured workflows. Choose based on the actual document and workflow requirements.

The honest answer

When each one is the right call.

Choose the signing process that fits the document, counterparties, and account requirements.

Native signature is fine for…

A native document process that fits your needs

  • Internal forms, opt-ins, and simple one-off consents
  • Documents where the counterparty doesn't care what tool signs them
  • Documents where the available recipient controls and signature certificate meet your process requirements

DocuSign (via LC Bridge) wins when…

An existing DocuSign process connected to the CRM

  • Your team already maintains its templates, recipients, and agreement records in DocuSign
  • The counterparty — a client, carrier, broker, or legal team — already runs on DocuSign
  • You want DocuSign agreement activity, mapped answers, and configured follow-up connected to GoHighLevel

Evaluate the process you already use

Some teams use GoHighLevel signing for one document process and DocuSign for another. If your client already relies on DocuSign, LC Bridge can connect that account to contact data and configured workflows. Check its account and setup requirements.
