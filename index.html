What does LC Bridge do?

LC Bridge builds products and custom software for GoHighLevel, also known as GoHighLevel. Our available products are DocuSign Integration and Community Agents. We also build integrations, marketplace apps, SaaS extensions, AI agents, and custom frontend experiences. Learn about the company.

Which product should I start with?

Choose DocuSign Integration if your team uses GoHighLevel and DocuSign for agreements. Choose Community Agents for AI replies and member context in GoHighLevel communities, including connected GoKollab portals. Each product has its own setup guide and pricing.

Can my agency offer these products to clients?

Yes, with product-specific account and branding requirements. DocuSign Agency and Scale plans support an agency-configured integration experience. Community Agents installs in the owning GoHighLevel sub-account, with subscription and usage billing through GoHighLevel. Review DocuSign plans and Community Agents billing before planning your rollout.

Do you build custom integrations for software companies?

Yes. We build GoHighLevel integrations and marketplace apps for software brands, and take on substantial agency projects. Start with a short brief about your product and the experience you want to create. We’ll discuss scope, architecture, deliverables, and support before work begins. Share your project.
