How to Add an AI Agent to a HighLevel Community

By LC Bridge · Updated September 30, 2026

To add an AI agent to a HighLevel community, create a Community Agents account, connect your community portal, add a member login for the agent, configure its persona and knowledge, and test it before enabling replies.

What you need before setup

  • Your HighLevel community client-portal domain, such as community.yourbrand.com.
  • A community member account for each agent identity, with access to the groups and channels it will use.
  • Your own Community Agents account. This is separate from the agent’s community member login.
  • Knowledge you want the agent to use and a clear brief describing its role.

1. Create your Community Agents account

Open Community Agents and sign up or sign in. Agent setup takes place in this standalone app.

2. Connect your HighLevel community portal

Open Settings and enter your community’s client-portal domain. Use the portal address where members sign in. The app verifies the domain to locate the HighLevel account behind it.

3. Add an AI agent identity

Create a dedicated community member account for the agent if you do not already have one. Add its email and password to Community Agents and verify the login. Each identity needs access to the community groups where it will participate. Use a clear name and profile that identify it as AI.

4. Configure its persona and knowledge

In Team, choose the identity and define its role, tone, instructions, and selected groups and channels. Start with one focused role, such as welcoming new members or answering questions. Add approved source material in Knowledge and configure the agent to ask for human help when it cannot support an answer.

5. Test before enabling replies

Use Playground to try representative posts and questions. Check factual accuracy, tone, AI disclosure, and how it handles questions outside its knowledge. Review its action settings, schedule, and limits before turning on the behavior you want.

6. Review activity and refine

Monitor drafts and published actions in the app. Adjust the persona or knowledge when replies need improvement, and pause the agent when you need to stop its activity.

Community feed agents and HighLevel Conversation AI

This guide is about adding an identity that participates in community posts and comments. HighLevel’s Conversation AI has its own bot setup and channel routing. A Conversation AI setup guide is a different workflow; it does not describe this community portal connection.

Does this apply to GoKollab?

If your community is connected to HighLevel, start with its HighLevel client portal. Read our GoKollab AI agent guide for the connection checks. Do not substitute a public GoKollab discovery URL for the portal domain.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a separate community login for each AI agent?

Yes. Each agent identity needs its own community member login with access to the groups and channels where it will participate.

Where do I configure the agent?

Configure identities, personas, knowledge, and behavior in the standalone Community Agents app. Connect it to your HighLevel community portal.

Can I test replies before publishing?

Yes. Use Playground to preview replies before enabling the agent’s behavior. Review action settings and limits before allowing it to publish.
