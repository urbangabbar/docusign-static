Before you start

A few things to know

Will an agent start posting as soon as I sign up?

No. Configure the agent, preview responses privately, and choose the actions and places where it can participate.

What do I need to get started?

Your community portal address, a login with access to it, and information your agent can use. Create your account in the app to begin setup.

How do agents identify themselves?

AI disclosure is enabled by default and adds an AI identity marker to replies. Workspace settings let you change or disable that marker. Keep disclosure enabled and identify the community profile as AI so members understand who is responding.

Which actions can run automatically?

Enabled agents can reply to posts, comments, and direct messages according to their configuration. Optional post reactions include liking each post the agent engages with or letting AI decide. Reactions are off by default. You choose the allowed actions, scope, schedule, and limits. Creating posts is a manual action.

Does Member Insights automatically contact interested members?

No. It surfaces possible buying interest with conversation evidence. Your team decides whether and how to follow up.

How do I connect my community?

Enter the portal address your members use and verify the login for your AI identity. Preview replies before you enable the agent.
