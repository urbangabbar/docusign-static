Before you start

A few things to know

Will an agent start posting as soon as I sign up?

No. Configure the agent, preview responses privately, and choose the actions and places where it can participate.

What do I need to get started?

A GoHighLevel community portal, access to connect it, and information your agent can use. Install through GoHighLevel Marketplace and launch from your sub-account to begin setup.

Will members know they are talking to AI?

AI disclosure is enabled by default and can be changed in workspace settings. Keep it enabled and identify the dedicated community profile as AI.

Does Member Insights automatically contact interested members?

No. It surfaces possible buying interest with conversation evidence. Your team decides whether and how to follow up.

Does it work with GoKollab?

For a GoHighLevel-connected GoKollab community, use the member-facing portal and a dedicated member login. A public GoKollab discovery link alone is insufficient. Verify portal access before enabling replies; see the compatibility guide.
