Monthly credit refreshes each billing period

Each successful monthly payment replaces your subscription allowance with $47. Unused subscription credit does not roll over. Trial credit expires when the trial ends.

Purchased credit carries over

Add extra AI credit from $2 when you need it. Purchased top-ups do not expire and remain separate from your monthly allowance. Usage spends expiring subscription credit first.

Stay in control of top-ups

Auto top-up is off by default. You can enable it in billing and choose your balance threshold, top-up amount, and monthly spending limit. Additional credit purchases are charged separately.

Before you subscribe
