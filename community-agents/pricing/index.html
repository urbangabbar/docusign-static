Pricing

Community Agents pricing for GoHighLevel

Subscribe in GoHighLevel Marketplace and launch directly from your sub-account. No separate Community Agents signup or checkout.

Standard

$47 USD / month / sub-account
  • $47 in AI usage credit with each successful monthly payment.
  • Additional AI usage billed through your GoHighLevel wallet.
  • Live agents and full buying-interest insights while subscribed.
  • Included usage and overage transaction history.
Install in GoHighLevel ↗

$47 USD per month per sub-account, plus applicable taxes. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled in GoHighLevel. Agencies may set their own resale pricing.

Monthly credit refreshes each billing period

Your active subscription includes $47 of AI usage each monthly billing period. Unused included credit expires when the allowance refreshes; it does not roll over.

Overage uses your GoHighLevel wallet

AI usage is measured at 4× provider cost. Your included allowance is spent first. Only usage above it is submitted to GoHighLevel Marketplace usage-based billing.

Manage billing in GoHighLevel

With agency rebilling enabled, overage charges use the sub-account wallet; otherwise they use the agency wallet. Manage wallet funding and usage limits in GoHighLevel. AI activity pauses if payment cannot complete.

Before you subscribe

Install Community Agents from GoHighLevel Marketplace for each sub-account you want to use. Your GoHighLevel session signs you into the app. Manage the recurring subscription, payment method and cancellation in GoHighLevel.

The included $47 is an AI usage allowance, not cash or a transferable wallet balance. It expires at the end of each billing period. Additional usage is charged separately through GoHighLevel. Agency resale markups and applicable taxes may affect the final amount you pay.

How the AI usage allowance works

Usage is metered at 4× provider cost. The examples below illustrate the billing calculation; they are not estimates of the messages an agent can send.

Provider cost in the periodMetered AI usageIncluded allowance usedAdditional usage charge
$10$40$40 of $47$0
$20$80$47$33 through the applicable GoHighLevel wallet

The $47 recurring subscription is paid separately from any overage. In the second example, subscription plus overage is $80 before taxes and agency resale pricing. Unused included credit expires when the monthly allowance refreshes. Actual usage depends on the model and work performed.
