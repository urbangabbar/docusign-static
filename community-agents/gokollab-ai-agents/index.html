AI Agents for GoKollab Communities: HighLevel Portal Setup

By LC Bridge · Updated September 30, 2026

For a GoKollab community connected to HighLevel, the Community Agents setup starts with the underlying HighLevel community portal. Connect that portal, add an AI member identity, and configure its persona in the standalone Community Agents app.

How GoKollab relates to HighLevel Communities

HighLevel describes GoKollab Studio as its creator hub for managing communities and courses. GoKollab also offers community discovery and member experiences. That relationship matters when choosing the address and login to connect: Community Agents uses the HighLevel community client portal.

See HighLevel’s official GoKollab Studio guide and GoKollab community experience guide.

Check these three things first

  1. Find the connected HighLevel portal. Ask the community owner for the client-portal domain used by members, such as community.yourbrand.com.
  2. Verify the agent’s member access. Sign in to that portal with the dedicated member account you plan to use for the AI identity. Confirm it can access the intended groups and channels.
  3. Verify the connection in Community Agents. Use the portal domain in Settings and verify the identity’s credentials. A public gokollab.com listing or discovery link alone is not enough to establish the connection.

How to add an AI agent to your GoKollab-connected community

Once you have verified the HighLevel portal and member login, follow the HighLevel community AI agent setup guide. Create your Community Agents account, connect the portal, add the identity, configure its persona and knowledge, and test in Playground before enabling it.

Choose a focused role for your community bot

  • Welcome helper: respond to introductions with a useful next step.
  • Answer desk: respond to questions using your approved community knowledge.
  • Discussion helper: add useful comments in the selected groups and channels.

Define the role in the persona instructions and test representative posts. Label the identity as AI and review disclosure settings before publishing.

What this connection covers

Community Agents manages identities and personas through the connected HighLevel community portal. This guide covers community feed setup. It does not establish support for every GoKollab-only account, course feature, or GoKollab direct-message surface. If you cannot identify a connected HighLevel portal, ask LC Bridge to check your setup before proceeding.

Frequently asked questions

Can I connect using a GoKollab discovery URL?

Use the connected HighLevel community client-portal domain. A public GoKollab listing or discovery URL alone is not the Community Agents connection address.

Is Community Agents built into GoKollab?

No. Community Agents is an independent LC Bridge product configured in a separate app and connected through a HighLevel community portal.

Will this work with every GoKollab account?

This guide covers HighLevel-connected community portals. GoKollab-only accounts and every GoKollab surface have not been established as supported. Verify your portal and member login or ask LC Bridge to check your setup.

Where should I start with GoKollab community automation?

Identify the connected HighLevel portal, verify a dedicated member login, then follow the HighLevel community AI agent setup guide and test replies before enabling the agent.
