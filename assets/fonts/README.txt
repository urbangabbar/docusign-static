Self-hosted font assets

Inter, Poppins and JetBrains Mono are distributed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1. The full copyright notices and licenses are retained alongside
the font files as inter-OFL.txt, poppins-OFL.txt and jetbrainsmono-OFL.txt.

Official license sources:
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/inter/OFL.txt
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/poppins/OFL.txt
https://github.com/google/fonts/blob/main/ofl/jetbrainsmono/OFL.txt

Existing Inter and Poppins 600/700 WOFF2 files were restored from lcbridge.app.
Poppins 500 and JetBrains Mono were obtained from the existing Google Fonts
dependency. The site loads Latin fonts locally with font-display: swap.
