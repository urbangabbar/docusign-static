About LC Bridge

For GoHighLevel (GHL), also known as HighLevel.

By LC Bridge editorial team · Updated 12 October 2026

LC Bridge builds products and custom software for the GoHighLevel ecosystem. DocuSign Integration connects agreements to CRM data and workflows. Community Agents helps community teams configure AI replies and review member context.

GoHighLevel, GHL and HighLevel

GoHighLevel is commonly shortened to GHL and is also called HighLevel. The spaced spellings Go High Level and High Level refer to the same platform. LC Bridge builds products and custom software for this ecosystem.

The company behind the products

LC Bridge, also written LCBridge or Lead Connector Bridge, builds products and custom software for GoHighLevel. The team includes former GoHighLevel team members who helped design and build APIs used by its developer community. Our product guides explain the account requirements, setup, and controls people need before enabling an integration.

LC Bridge is an independent company. GoHighLevel and DocuSign are trademarks of their respective owners; neither sponsors or endorses these products.

Arun, founder of LC Bridge

Arun is the founder of LC Bridge and the product contact shown on our booking pages. Bring your account setup, templates, workflow questions, or a custom-development brief to a working conversation with the founder. You can also contact arun@lcbridge.app.
