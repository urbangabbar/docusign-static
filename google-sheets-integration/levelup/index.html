Questions

Good to know

When can I start using it?

We're opening in the coming weeks. Everyone with early access gets their access link first, by email.

What will it cost?

Usage-based: 250 record transfers a month are included, then from $0.015 per record, cheaper at volume. Early access is free.

Is my CRM data safe?

Nothing changes in your CRM without your review. Every update shows the old and new values before you confirm it.

Does it work with a white-labelled HighLevel account?

Yes. It's built for HighLevel, including agency white-label setups.

Do I need to install anything?

It runs inside Google Sheets. You connect your HighLevel account and a Google account, then choose what to bring in.
