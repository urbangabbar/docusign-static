Questions

Good to know

When can I start using it?

We're opening in November. Everyone with early access gets their access link first, by email.

What will it cost?

Early access is free and needs no credit card. After launch, 250 record transfers a month are included free, then from $0.015 per record, cheaper at volume. Or choose Unlimited at $59 a month.

Is my CRM data safe?

Nothing changes in your CRM without your review. Every update shows the old and new values before you confirm it.

Does it work with a white-labelled HighLevel account?

Yes. It's built for HighLevel, including agency white-label setups.

Do I need to install anything?

It runs inside Google Sheets. You connect your HighLevel account and a Google account, then choose what to bring in.

Who makes it?

LC Bridge. We build apps for HighLevel, including Community Agents and the DocuSign integration, both available now.
