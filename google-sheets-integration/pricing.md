# Google Sheets integration by LC Bridge — planned early-access pricing

Public pricing: https://lcbridge.app/google-sheets-integration/pricing/
Currency: USD. Base rates before applicable markup and tax.
Unit: each successfully transferred primary record on each execution.
250 record transfers included per connected account per calendar month (UTC).
Volume discounts accumulate per connected account in that month.

## Graduated rates after the allowance
- First 1,000 billable transfers: $0.015 per transfer.
- Next 1,000: $0.012 per transfer.
- Next 3,000: $0.009 per transfer.
- Next 25,000: $0.0075 per transfer.
- Above 30,000 billable transfers: $0.0045 per transfer.

## Counting rules
- Each execution counts its successfully transferred primary records. Transferring the same record in another execution adds usage again.
- Retries of the same delivery operation must not create duplicate charges.
- Supporting lookups and embedded invoice or order line items are bundled with their primary records.
- Refreshing a report from already delivered worksheet data is not another provider record transfer. A source refresh that transfers records adds usage.
- Applicable account markup and tax can change the final charge.
- High-volume teams can discuss a custom subscription with more predictable costs.
