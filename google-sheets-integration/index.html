Questions before you connect.

Can I connect GoHighLevel to Google Sheets?

Yes: native CSV and Google Sheets workflow actions serve different data-movement needs. LC Bridge is preparing a dedicated early-access connection for configured imports, reports, and reviewed updates.

Is this the same product as CRM Sync?

Yes. CRM Sync is the in-app name. The public integration is Google Sheets integration for GoHighLevel by LC Bridge.

Does editing a cell automatically update the CRM?

No. Eligible outgoing updates to existing Contacts and Opportunities require a change preview and explicit confirmation. Other object writeback is not promised.

Are the dashboards live customer data?

No. All public previews use invented data and a fixed sample period. They do not connect to accounts or change CRM records.

How does pricing work?

Planned rates charge per successfully transferred primary record on each execution, with a monthly allowance and graduated volume discounts. A repeat execution transferring the same records adds usage.

Can I get a predictable subscription for large volumes?

Yes. Talk to Sales about a custom subscription based on the volume and frequency your team needs.

How do I request access?

Use the existing Google Sheets waitlist below. Joining requests early access; it does not install the app, connect an account, or authorize charges.
