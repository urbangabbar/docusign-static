FAQ

Questions before you connect.

Can I connect GoHighLevel to Google Sheets?

Yes. CSV exports and native Google Sheets workflow actions cover one-off and event-driven jobs. LC Bridge adds scheduled imports, 25 report templates, integrations and reviewed updates. Compare the methods.

Does editing a cell update the CRM automatically?

No. Changes to existing contacts and opportunities are previewed first and sent only when you confirm. Other record types are import-only.

Is it white-labelled for my clients?

Yes. Inside the app, the CRM is called "your CRM", buttons say "Open in CRM", and HighLevel is never named in the app, its statuses or its errors.

Which integrations are included?

Facebook Ads, Google Ads, LinkedIn Ads, CallRail, Stripe and QuickBooks, joined to your contacts by recorded IDs. We add more every month based on customer feedback.

Are the dashboards on this site live customer data?

No. Every public preview uses invented sample data frozen to October 2026. It never connects to an account.

How does pricing work?

You pay per record transferred on each run, after 250 free transfers a month, with automatic volume discounts. Running the same records again counts again.

Can I get a predictable subscription for large volumes?

Yes. Talk to Sales about a custom subscription for the volume and frequency your team needs.
