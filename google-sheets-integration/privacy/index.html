Google Sheets early-access privacy notice

How the public LC Bridge Google Sheets website, sample previews, and existing waitlist handle your information.

What this website collects

You can explore the public report previews without submitting personal information. The previews use invented records. They do not request a CRM token, connect a Google account, or read your spreadsheet.

The existing hosted waitlist asks for your email address to manage your Google Sheets early-access request. It does not ask for a phone number or CRM account credentials. If you choose to book a sales conversation, that separate booking form collects the details shown there. Submit only information you want LC Bridge to receive.

How we use a request

LC Bridge uses the details you submit to respond to your Google Sheets integration inquiry, coordinate early access, and understand your stated requirements. Joining the waitlist requests product access and follow-up about that request. We do not claim that visiting a page signs you up or that an iframe load confirms a submission.

The waitlist is provided through HighLevel/LeadConnector, either its hosted form or, on the campaign page, a direct submission to an LC Bridge HighLevel workflow. A sales booking uses the existing booking provider. Those services receive the information you submit to them and operate their own hosting and processing infrastructure.

Analytics and local sample controls

Apart from the campaign-page pixel described below, the site’s analytics adapter requires a configured measurement stream and analytics consent before sending analytics requests. Event properties are limited to page paths and documented interaction categories; form values, customer records, and private query strings are not added to analytics events.

Sample filters recalculate invented data locally in your browser. They are not uploaded as customer datasets. External hosted forms can have their own storage and cookie behavior, which is governed by the provider’s interface and notices.

Advertising measurement on the campaign page

The LevelUp campaign page (/google-sheets-integration/levelup/) loads the Meta pixel by default to measure visits from our Meta ads. It records that the page was viewed, that a template link was clicked, and that an early-access request was sent. It does not send your email address or form answers to Meta. Meta may set cookies and processes this data under its own policies.

To turn it off, open Your privacy choices in the site footer and choose Keep analytics off. The pixel stops on that page and does not load on later visits from the same browser. Browser tracking protection also blocks it. No other page in this section loads the Meta pixel.

Visitor identification

Every page on lcbridge.app loads RB2B by default. RB2B uses cookies and your IP address to try to match a visit to a business profile, such as a name, company and LinkedIn page, so we can follow up with interested businesses. It does not read form answers.

The website privacy policy describes RB2B, the Meta pixel, retention, and how to opt out or request your data. Keep analytics off in the footer’s Your privacy choices stops both, and so does a Global Privacy Control signal.

Requests and production access

For a question about your submitted information, correction, or deletion request, contact support@lcbridge.app. Include enough information to identify your own request, without sending passwords, tokens, or unrelated customer data.

This notice covers the public early-access website and inquiry flow. Connected integration permissions, actual data processing, retention, and billing terms must be supplied through the production access process before you connect real CRM or spreadsheet data.
