What the metrics mean

Recorded sales won

Record grain: opportunity

Date basis: Latest qualifying status timestamp

Rules: Won only; reopened deals move historical results; missing values and other currencies excluded from sum

Sales goal

Record grain: goal period

Date basis: Monthly target period

Rules: Configured unit/currency matches actual

Goal achieved

Record grain: opportunity + configured goal

Date basis: Outcome date in goal month

Rules: Actual / goal; not clamped at 100%

Remaining to goal

Record grain: opportunity + configured goal

Date basis: Outcome date in goal month

Rules: max(goal - actual, 0); excess separate

Required data and boundaries

  • Opportunities

Goals have a period, unit, and currency. A missing or incompatible goal is not a zero target; collected cash is measured by payment reports.

Targets apply to the full goal month. Actuals use the selected outcome dates. Open pipeline is as of the latest refresh.

Latest-status basis: reopened deals can change historical won/lost results. Fixed historical outcomes require validated event history. Recorded sales won is deal value, not collected revenue.
