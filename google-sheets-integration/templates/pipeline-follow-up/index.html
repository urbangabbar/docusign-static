What the metrics mean

Open deals

Record grain: opportunity

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Open status only; abandoned, lost and won excluded

Open deal value

Record grain: opportunity

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Sum known values in reporting currency; missing values disclosed

Deals over stage-age limit

Record grain: opportunity

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Valid latest stage-entry timestamp required; threshold configured per stage

Deals with missing owner

Record grain: opportunity

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Open opportunities without owner

Required data and boundaries

  • Opportunities
  • Contact tasks

Stage bars are current-state snapshots. Historical stage conversion belongs in a separate history-based report; missing values and missing owners remain inspectable.

Current stage distribution is not historical conversion. Tasks are linked only by explicit opportunity ID.

Latest-status basis: reopened deals can change historical won/lost results. Fixed historical outcomes require validated event history. Recorded sales won is deal value, not collected revenue.
