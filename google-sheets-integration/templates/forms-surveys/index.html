Required data and boundaries

  • Form/survey submissions
  • Contacts
  • Opportunities

Submission volume does not establish visitor-to-lead conversion. Form and survey identities stay distinct, and missing contact matches remain visible.

Repeated submissions remain distinct. Contact counts deduplicate by ID; opportunities remain separate facts. No visitor denominator, conversion rate, survey score or causal revenue attribution is manufactured.
