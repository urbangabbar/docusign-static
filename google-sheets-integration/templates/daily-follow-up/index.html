What the metrics mean

Overdue tasks

Record grain: task

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Incomplete tasks with due day before local today

Tasks due today

Record grain: task

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Incomplete tasks due on local today

Appointments today

Record grain: appointment occurrence

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Today, not cancelled or superseded

Deals over stage-age limit

Record grain: opportunity

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Valid latest stage-entry timestamp required; threshold configured per stage

Required data and boundaries

  • Contact tasks
  • Appointments and calendar scope
  • Opportunities
  • Contacts

This is an as-of operational queue. Generic update timestamps are not task deadlines, and a completed flag is not proof of completion during a historical period.
