What the metrics mean

Successful live collections

Record grain: transaction

Date basis: Validated payment date

Rules: Successful live transactions in selected currency; gross, refunds separate

Current overdue amount

Record grain: invoice

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Eligible unpaid balance past due date in selected currency

Overdue invoices

Record grain: invoice

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Eligible unpaid invoices past due; one invoice per ID

Payments with unresolved links

Record grain: transaction

Date basis: Payment date

Rules: Successful live collections missing explicit opportunity relationship

Required data and boundaries

  • Successful live transactions
  • Invoices and current balances
  • Contacts
  • Opportunities

Gross live collections are not recognized revenue. Current overdue balances are snapshots, and a shared contact does not allocate a payment to a deal.

CRM financial coverage only · USD. Gross collections; $200 recorded refunded separately. 1 other-currency and 1 test transactions excluded. A contact match never attributes a payment to a deal.
