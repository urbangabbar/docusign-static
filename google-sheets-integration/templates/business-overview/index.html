What the metrics mean

New leads

Record grain: contact

Date basis: Contact created date

Rules: Validated lead definition; contacts without it use New contacts

Recorded sales won

Record grain: opportunity

Date basis: Latest qualifying status timestamp

Rules: Won only; reopened deals move historical results; missing values and other currencies excluded from sum

Appointments attended

Record grain: appointment occurrence

Date basis: Appointment start date

Rules: Validated Attended status

Overdue follow-ups

Record grain: task

Date basis: As of latest refresh

Rules: Incomplete tasks with due day before local today

Required data and boundaries

  • Contacts
  • Opportunities
  • Appointments and calendar scope
  • Contact tasks

The sample combines a sales trend with top-customer ranking. Its operational counters remain separate facts; it does not label won deals as accounting revenue.

Latest-status basis: reopened deals can change historical won/lost results. Fixed historical outcomes require validated event history. Recorded sales won is deal value, not collected revenue.
