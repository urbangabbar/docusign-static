Google Sheets early-access terms

What the public samples, waitlist request, planned prices, and production access process mean.

Early access and public samples

The website describes Google Sheets integration for GoHighLevel by LC Bridge and provides synthetic report previews. Those previews illustrate the reviewed report calculations and arrangements. They are not a live connection, installation, or promise that every source is enabled in a particular account.

Joining the waitlist requests early access. It does not authorize an installation, grant data permissions, start a subscription, or permit charges. LC Bridge will communicate the applicable access process separately.

Planned pricing and estimates

Published usage rates are planned early-access base rates in USD. The billable unit is each successfully transferred primary record on an execution. A new execution transferring the same records creates additional usage. Monthly allowances and graduated bands describe accumulation periods, not monthly record deduplication.

The calculator provides an estimate and does not settle charges. Applicable markup and tax can change the final amount. Actual production use requires the applicable rates and billing authorization. Custom subscriptions are negotiated separately and do not imply an unlimited entitlement.

Use of the website

Use the previews to evaluate reporting concepts with their included synthetic records. Do not submit passwords, provider tokens, sensitive customer exports, or other account credentials through the waitlist or ordinary sales inquiries.

Follow the source platform’s current requirements when using a native import or workflow method described in a guide. Verify the intended account, record identity, mapping, permissions, and data before any real operation.

Support and changes

Contact support@lcbridge.app with website or early-access questions. We may update the previews and guides as the product and source capabilities change. Meaningful product and pricing facts are identified by their review date.

Google Sheets and GoHighLevel are third-party products. Their names describe compatibility and do not imply that LC Bridge is owned, operated, or endorsed by either provider.
